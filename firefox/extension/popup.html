How to interpret these calculations

Axis error = (measured − target) ÷ target × 100. A minus sign means smaller than the target. Axis gap is the absolute difference between the signed X and Y errors.

The two flags answer separate questions. Both axes can be within the chosen threshold while their gap exceeds it. Equal undersized axes can have a zero gap and still fail the axis threshold.

No input is a physical test supplied by Sewlore. This tool does not assess fabric quality, compliance, fit or measurement uncertainty. It does not recommend one printer correction for unequal axes.

Results use JavaScript floating-point arithmetic and up to nine significant figures on screen. CSV keeps the available numeric precision. Extremely large or underflowing conversions are rejected; small rounding differences remain possible.
